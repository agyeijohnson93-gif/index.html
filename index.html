Frequently asked questions

How do I choose a size or color?

Open a product and select any available size, color or option shown before adding it to your cart.

Where can I find my orders?

Click Orders in the navigation. After checkout, your order reference is saved in this browser for easy tracking.

How can I contact customer care?

Click Customer Care below. The store email is intentionally not displayed on the public page.

Can products be returned?

Return rules are controlled by the store. The admin can edit the store policy and customer-care information from Manage Store.
